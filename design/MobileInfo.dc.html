Overview

Drivers Against Flock ("DAF," "we," "us") is a privacy-focused navigation app operated by LayeredTech, LLC. It is designed to collect only the information needed to provide app features.

The app helps you search for places, view maps, get directions, and choose routes with privacy in mind. DAF works with or without location access — the app stays usable if you deny it.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not build advertising profiles about users.
