Drivers Against Flock — Watch app

The complete wrist app in its final state, Wear OS (round) and Apple Watch side by side. The watch reads what the phone knows: it shows guidance, camera alerts, the live Scorecard and a 20-second recap during a routed drive, and your watched Areas when idle. One cross-device verb — "Open on phone" / "Open on iPhone". Tokens, components, system states, flows and the haptic vocabulary live on Foundations.

When the cue fires
The advance cue plays once per maneuver when ETA ≤ 10 s or distance ≤ 400 ft, whichever comes first — so it lands at the same "look up now" moment at 25 mph and at 65 mph. Never re-fires after a reroute to the same maneuver.
Visual state at cue time
Distance turns brand green and a 2dp ring pulses once around the glyph (420 ms, `--ease-soft`). In ambient, the haptic still plays and the display wakes to interactive for 5 s.
After the maneuver
The next step slides in from the right (240 ms). No haptic — the next maneuver earns its own cue only when it reaches its own threshold. "Now" at ≤ 60 ft is visual only.

19 · Cue priority & suppression — closely spaced maneuvers
{{ r.title }}
{{ r.body }}
Rules, in order
1 · One cue per maneuver, ever. A reroute that keeps the same maneuver does not re-cue.
2 · If the next maneuver's threshold would fire within 2.5 s of the current cue, the two merge: the current cue plays, the visual shows "then ↱" as a secondary glyph (35%), and the second cue is suppressed.
3 · A cue older than 2 s that hasn't played (phone lag, stale data, DND window) is dropped. Late cues are worse than no cues.
4 · Maneuver cues outrank everything else on the wrist: camera alerts and Area changes wait for a 250 ms gap or are dropped.
5 · Suppressed cues are logged (`cue_suppressed: merged | stale | dnd | disabled`) and surface in Diagnostics for device testing.
20 · Camera alert priority — the turn cue always wins
{{ r.title }}
{{ r.body }}
Priority order on the wrist
1 · Maneuver cue (§7) — always first, never deferred.
2 · Camera alert — deferred to the next 250 ms gap; expires when the camera is passed or the alert is > 2 s late.
3 · Arrived / Lost — play when they happen; they can't collide with a maneuver by definition.
4 · Area change (§19) — idle only; silently dropped during any route.
Visual priority mirrors haptic priority: the maneuver block owns the top; camera owns the trip-strip slot; nothing else draws during a route.
