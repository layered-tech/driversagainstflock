Timing rules
Minimum 250 ms silence between any two cues. A cue never plays more than 2 s after its trigger — late cues are dropped, not queued. Maneuver cues outrank camera cues, which outrank Area cues; a lower cue that collides is suppressed, not delayed.
Platform notes
Wear uses `VibrationEffect` compositions with amplitude; Apple uses `WKInterfaceDevice.play(_:)`, which is fixed-amplitude — direction is conveyed by count and type, never by strength. Both honor system silent/DND and the in-app toggle (Phase 2).
Accessibility
Every haptic has a visible equivalent on screen at the same instant. Haptics never carry information alone. Patterns are previewable from Settings so drivers can learn them parked.
H · Truncation, touch, accessibility
Truncation
Road names: drop the type suffix first ("Market St" → "Market"), then middle-ellipsize at 2 lines. Area names: tail-ellipsize at 1 line. Numbers never truncate — the unit shrinks or drops instead.
Touch
Wear 48dp min, 52dp for in-drive; Apple 44pt. In-drive screens have at most one tappable region; everything else is swipe/crown. No double-tap or long-press for anything safety-related.
Accessibility
Large-text scales up to 125% on Wear and the `xxxLarge` Dynamic Type step on Apple — layouts drop the trip strip before shrinking type. Screen readers announce maneuver changes (`liveRegion` / `.updatesFrequently`). Color never carries meaning alone: tone is paired with glyph and label.
