  1. Back is the system chevron + parent title in brand green (`NavigationStack`). Edge-swipe also goes back. No custom back buttons anywhere.
  2. Crown scrolls every list and the recap; the scroll indicator is the system's. `.digitalCrownRotation` is not used for anything custom — in-drive screens don't scroll at all.

What's preserved
Count carries direction (1 = left, 2 = right); a longer, softer system pattern means neutral; three quick ticks mean camera. The meanings and the 250 ms / 2 s timing rules are identical to Wear.
What differs, and why
`WKInterfaceDevice.play(_:)` has no amplitude or duration control, so "long" and "soft" are approximated by system types rather than specified in ms. Traces above show the system patterns' approximate envelopes. Sequences are scheduled with `Timer` on the main actor; a second `play` within ~100 ms can be coalesced by the system, so Right uses 180 ms.
Background limits
Haptics play only while the app is frontmost or during a notification. The Smart Stack pin plus `.timeSensitive` notifications for maneuver cues cover the backgrounded case; if the app is suspended, the cue arrives as a notification haptic (`.notification`) with the maneuver in the short look — directional meaning is lost and the screen does the work. This is the one accepted degradation; it is logged as `cue_fallback: notification`.
