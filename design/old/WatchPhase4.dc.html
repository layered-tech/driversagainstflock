Back behavior
Wear: swipe-right goes up one level (change → Area → home → watch face). Apple: system back chevron in the title; the crown scrolls lists.
Route interrupts
If a route starts while the driver is anywhere in My areas, the stack is discarded and Active navigation takes the screen. After the route (and the Phase 5 recap) the app returns to the My areas home, not to the previous depth.
Stays on the phone
Creating an Area, editing its boundary, the map, camera photos, and reporting. The watch reads; it never writes.
