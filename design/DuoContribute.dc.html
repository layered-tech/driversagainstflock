OpenStreetMap editing · Duo

One changeset, many changes — the OSM model. Open a changeset from the base map, then work the map for as long as you like: add new cameras, edit or delete existing ones. Every change is staged in a tray at the bottom. When you're done, describe the changeset once, review the full diff, and publish — or close the editor and keep everything as a draft; the pencil on the map carries the pending count until you come back.
