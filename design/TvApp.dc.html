Drivers Against Flock — Android TV

The one surface where DAF only watches. On a TV it's a lean-back, shared-household screen driven by a D-pad, and it does one thing: show the areas you watch and what changed in them this week. No search, no routing, no contributing — all of that stays on the phone. There's no account, so the phone pairs over home Wi-Fi and syncs its areas straight to the TV; nothing goes through us. Drawn at 960 × 540 dp (1080p at 2×), 48 / 27 dp overscan margins, focus = scale + Signal Green ring, nothing smaller than 12 dp type.
