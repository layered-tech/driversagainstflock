Arrived (3 s)
Phase 1's hold, unchanged: flag glyph, "Arrived", haptic Arrived. During these 3 s the phone finalizes metrics; if it hasn't by the end, the recap opens in its Unavailable-metrics variant and upgrades in place when numbers arrive.
Recap (≤ 20 s)
Scroll or tap cancels auto-close. Dismiss: swipe-right (Wear) / back (Apple), or the countdown. "Open on phone" also closes the recap after a successful handoff.
Back to idle
Returns to My areas home with the stack reset. Tile and complications refresh immediately. The recap is not revisitable on the watch — history lives in the phone's Scorecard.
